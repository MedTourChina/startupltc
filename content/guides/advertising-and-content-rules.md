Most foreign founders write marketing copy in English and translate it. The translation is usually accurate and still illegal, because the rules are not about truth — they are about specific words, specific products and specific disclosure.

## The absolute-claim ban

Article 9 of the Advertising Law prohibits terms such as 国家级 (national-level), 最高级 (highest-grade) and 最佳 (best), and anything that implies the same.

In practice this rules out:

- "The best", "No.1", "the leading", "the most advanced", "the only".
- "National-level", "state-approved", "official".
- Comparisons that put a competitor down without a substantiated basis.
- Claims that a product or service is endorsed by a government body or a national project.

"Top-quality", "premium" and "flagship" are generally acceptable; "the top" is not. The line is fuzzy in translation and strict in enforcement, and e-commerce platforms police it themselves.

:::warn
Platforms remove listings and fine merchants for banned terms before a regulator ever sees the ad. A product page that says "best in the world" is a takedown, a penalty deducted from the deposit, and a week of lost sales.
:::

## Products that need approval before advertising

Some categories cannot advertise at all, and some need a pre-advertising review certificate (广告审查证明) issued by the health or drug authority:

| Category | Rule |
|---|---|
| Prescription drugs | Advertising to the public prohibited |
| Non-prescription drugs | Pre-approval required, certificate number displayed |
| Medical devices | Pre-approval required for most classes |
| Medical services | Pre-approval required; results claims restricted |
| Health foods | Pre-approval, and no disease-treatment claims |
| Formula milk for infants under 1 | Advertising of the product prohibited in media aimed at the public |
| Tobacco | Prohibited, including vape products |
| Alcohol | Restricted placement and no encouragement of excessive drinking |
| Education and training | No promises of results, no guarantees of scores or admission, no use of exam candidates |
| Real estate | Price, area and permit details must be accurate; no升值 promises |
| Financial products | Risk warnings, no guaranteed returns |

If the product is in one of these categories, the ad cannot run until the certificate exists, and the certificate number must appear in the ad. This is a pre-launch dependency, not a compliance detail to fix later.

## Online advertising

- Paid content must be identifiable as advertising. Native content, influencer posts and search ads all need a clear label — commonly 广告.
- Pop-ups must have a visible close button.
- Ads must not interfere with normal use of the app or browser, and must not be disguised as system messages.
- Platforms are responsible for the ads they carry, which is why they remove risky listings quickly.

Influencer marketing (KOL, 达人) is advertising. The endorser must have actually used the product where the law requires it, medical and pharmaceutical products cannot be endorsed by patients or experts in a misleading way, and the brand is liable for claims the influencer makes on its behalf.

## Live commerce

Livestream selling is treated as advertising plus sales. The streamer's claims bind the merchant, the platform has review duties, and recordings are evidence. Scripts should be reviewed like ad copy — including the improvised parts.

## Content that is not advertising

Separately from advertising, internet content is subject to the prohibited-content rules: no content that endangers national security, incites separatism, spreads rumours, obscene material, violence, or insults a protected group. Platforms apply these rules aggressively, and a user-generated feature without moderation is a liability, not a feature.

## Penalties

- False advertising: three to five times the advertising fees, minimum ¥200,000; serious cases up to ten times and revocation.
- Banned absolute terms: correction order and fines, typically ¥200,000–1,000,000 for the advertiser in serious cases.
- Advertising a product that requires pre-approval without one: fines and removal.
- Endorser liability: joint liability where the endorser knew or should have known.

The pattern is that the fine is calibrated to the ad spend, so a small company running a large campaign can face a penalty larger than its revenue.

## A review that costs an hour

1. Search the copy for absolute claims, in Chinese and in the original.
2. Check whether the product category requires pre-approval, and whether the certificate exists.
3. Confirm the label on every paid placement.
4. Check influencer scripts and the contract's claim warranties.
5. Keep substantiation for every factual claim — test reports, certifications, survey data.

:::checklist
- No 国家级 / 最高级 / 最佳 or their equivalents in any asset
- Category pre-approval obtained and certificate number displayed
- Paid placements labelled as advertising
- Influencer contracts require compliance with the Advertising Law
- Claims substantiated with documents you could show an inspector
- Live-commerce scripts reviewed, not improvised
:::
