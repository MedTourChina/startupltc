Healthcare splits into two very different businesses: providing care, which is restricted, and making or selling products, which is open but heavily regulated.

## Providing care

The negative list keeps medical institutions at "limited to equity joint venture" — a Chinese partner is required, and the foreign stake is capped. On top of the negative list, the health commission's own rules apply:

- A 设置医疗机构批准书 (approval to establish) before construction or leasing.
- A 医疗机构执业许可证 (practical licence) before the first patient.
- Department scope, bed count, equipment list and staffing approved in advance.
- Doctors registered at the institution; a doctor may not practise at an unregistered site.

In September 2024 China opened a pilot allowing wholly foreign-owned hospitals in Beijing, Tianjin, Shanghai, Nanjing, Suzhou, Fuzhou, Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Hainan. The pilot excludes psychiatric, infectious-disease and traditional Chinese medicine hospitals, and does not permit acquiring public hospitals. Outside those places, and outside the pilot's scope, the JV rule stands.

For a clinic rather than a hospital, 2022 rules replaced approval with filing (诊所备案) for domestic operators — but a foreign-invested clinic still needs the establishment approval and the JV structure. Filing is not an exemption from the foreign investment rules.

:::warn
"we will open a small clinic first, then expand" is the most expensive sentence in this sector. The premises, the department scope and the equipment list are approved together, and changing any of them later means a variation application and, often, a new fit-out.
:::

## Telemedicine

Internet hospitals (互联网医院) are licensed as an extension of a physical institution, not as a standalone app:

- A physical medical institution must be the依托 (anchor), and the internet hospital is registered against it.
- Online consultation is limited to follow-up visits for common and chronic diseases — no first diagnosis.
- Prescriptions are limited, and certain drugs cannot be prescribed online.
- Records must be retained, and the platform is audited as part of the institution.

A foreign company can build the software for a licensed institution; it cannot be the institution.

## Medical devices

Devices are open to wholly foreign-owned companies, and the gate is product registration with NMPA.

| Class | Examples | Route | Typical time |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | Bandages, surgical instruments, stethoscopes | Filing (备案) with the provincial NMPA | 1–3 months |
| II | Ultrasound, monitors, most IVD reagents | Registration with the provincial NMPA | 12–18 months |
| III | Implants, high-risk devices, some IVD | Registration with NMPA in Beijing | 18–36 months |

Requirements:

- A **domestic agent** (境内代理人) registered in China, responsible for the registration and post-market duties.
- A quality management system that meets China's GMP, with a possible on-site audit of the overseas factory.
- A clinical evaluation — either an exemption under the catalogue, a literature-based evaluation, or a clinical trial.
- Chinese labelling and instructions, with a unique device identifier where required.
- Registration certificate renewal before expiry, and change filings for modifications.

Selling is a second licence: Class II distribution needs filing (经营备案), Class III needs a distribution licence (经营许可证), and the premises, storage and quality staff are inspected.

## Drugs

- Clinical trials need approval (with a 60-working-day implied-approval mechanism for some categories).
- Marketing requires a drug registration certificate; the holder can be overseas with a domestic agent.
- Imported drugs enter through designated ports with import recordal.
- Distribution needs a drug distribution licence, and online sales are restricted to listed categories.

## The realistic sequence

1. Decide which business you are in: care provider, device maker, distributor, or software vendor.
2. For care: partner, city (pilot or not), premises, and health commission pre-consultation — in that order.
3. For devices: classify the product, appoint the domestic agent, start the clinical evaluation and the QMS audit in parallel.
4. Build the distribution licences before the first shipment, not after.
5. Budget regulatory affairs as a permanent function, not a one-off project.

:::checklist
- Sector position confirmed: provider, manufacturer, distributor or software
- If provider: JV partner and city checked against the 2024 pilot
- Device classification confirmed with NMPA, not guessed
- Domestic agent appointed before registration
- Clinical evaluation route documented
- Distribution licences in place before first sale
- Labelling and instructions in Chinese, reviewed
:::
