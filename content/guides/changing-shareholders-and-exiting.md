Most founders think about exit as a term sheet event. In China it is an administrative process with a tax bill and a public record, and the difference between a clean exit and a damaged one is usually whether the company was kept in order beforehand.

## The four exits

| Exit | What happens | Timeline | Tax |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Equity transfer | Shares sold to an existing or new shareholder | 3–8 weeks | 20% IIT (individual) or 10% withholding (foreign company) on the gain; 0.05% stamp duty |
| Capital reduction | Capital returned to shareholders | 2–4 months | Treated as dividend or capital return depending on the amount |
| Liquidation and deregistration | Company wound up, assets distributed, registration cancelled | 3–6 months | Tax clearance required; withholding on distributions above capital |
| Abandonment | No filings, no deregistration | — | Licence revoked, legal representative restricted |

The first three are exits. The fourth is damage control.

## Equity transfer

Under the Company Law as revised in 2023, a shareholder can transfer equity to a non-shareholder after **written notice** to the other shareholders of the quantity, price, payment terms and deadline. The other shareholders have a **right of first refusal** on the same terms and must respond within **30 days**, or the right is treated as waived. The articles can set different rules.

The process:

1. Board and shareholder resolutions, and waivers of pre-emptive rights.
2. A transfer agreement with price, payment schedule, representations and warranties.
3. Tax filing and payment — 20% IIT on the gain for an individual seller; 10% withholding for a foreign corporate seller, subject to treaty relief.
4. Registry change within **30 days** of the transfer.
5. Foreign-investment information report, where the shareholder is foreign.
6. Update the bank mandate, the foreign-exchange registration and the capital account records.

:::warn
The registry will check that the transfer price is not obviously below fair value. A sale at par to a related party when the company has retained profits invites a tax adjustment based on the net asset value. Get a valuation, or be ready to justify the price.
:::

## Capital reduction

Reducing registered capital returns money to shareholders without selling the company. It is also how a company corrects an over-sized capital commitment.

The procedure under the 2023 Company Law:

1. Shareholder resolution and amended articles.
2. Balance sheet and asset inventory prepared.
3. Creditors notified within **10 days**, and a public announcement within **30 days**.
4. Creditors may demand repayment or security within 30 days of notice, or 45 days of the announcement.
5. Registration of the change, then the remittance abroad with tax clearance.

The waiting period is the point: creditors get a chance to object. Plan two to four months.

## Liquidation and deregistration

A general liquidation runs as follows:

| Step | Timing | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resolution to dissolve | Day 0 | Shareholder resolution |
| Liquidation group formed | Within 15 days | Filing with the registry |
| Creditors notified | Within 10 days of formation | Written notices |
| Public announcement | Within 60 days | Newspaper or national credit system |
| Tax clearance | During liquidation | Tax deregistration certificate |
| Asset distribution | After debts and taxes | Liquidation report |
| Deregistration | After clearance | Cancelled licence |
| Bank accounts closed, seals cancelled | After deregistration | Closure confirmations |

The **simplified deregistration** route is faster: a public notice of **20 days**, an undertaking by all investors that there are no outstanding debts, and no liquidation group. It is available only to companies that meet the conditions — no operations, no creditors, no异常 listing.

:::note
Deregistration does not extinguish personal guarantees, unpaid social insurance or tax liabilities. The tax clearance is the gate, and it will surface anything the company failed to file.
:::

## Abandonment

A company that stops filing but is never deregistered does not disappear:

- The market regulator lists it as abnormal, then revokes the licence (吊销).
- The legal representative is barred from serving as a director or senior manager of another company for **three years**.
- The company's record stays public, and the shareholders remain liable for unpaid capital.
- The bank account cannot be closed cleanly, and the tax bureau can pursue the company and, in some cases, the representative.

For a foreign founder, the three-year bar and the public record are the practical costs. The company is not a disposable shell.

## Before you exit

Whatever the route, the same file matters:

- Audited accounts and complete tax filings.
- Social insurance and housing fund records.
- Contracts assigned or terminated, leases surrendered, employees settled.
- Bank accounts reconciled and closed.
- Seals and licences surrendered or cancelled.
- Shareholder and director resolutions documenting each step.

A company that has kept its filings can exit in months. A company that has not spends the first months reconstructing its own history.

:::checklist
- Exit route chosen and modelled: transfer, reduction, liquidation or simplified deregistration
- Shareholder resolutions and waivers signed
- Valuation prepared if the price is below net asset value
- Tax computed on the gain and filing dates booked
- Creditor notice and announcement periods scheduled
- Employees, lease and suppliers settled before deregistration
- Bank accounts closed and seals cancelled
- Foreign-investment information report filed
- Legal representative's role handed over or terminated on the registry
:::

:::rule
There is no informal exit in China. Transfer, reduce, liquidate or abandon — and only the first three leave you free to start again.
:::
