Restaurants look like the most accessible business in China — everyone eats, the model is obvious, and the sector is not on the negative list. The failure rate comes from the premises, not the cooking.

## Who may own it

Food and beverage service is open. A foreign founder can hold 100% of a restaurant company, and no sector approval is needed at the investment stage. The company's business scope includes 餐饮服务, and the licences are issued locally.

:::warn
"Open to foreign ownership" does not mean "easy to license". A restaurant in a residential building, a mall unit without a grease trap, or a kitchen smaller than the local standard will fail the food licence no matter how good the food is.
:::

## The four approvals

| Approval | Issued by | What it checks |
|---|---|---|
| Business licence with 餐饮服务 scope | Market supervision bureau | Company, address, scope |
| Food business licence (食品经营许可证) | District market supervision bureau | Kitchen layout, equipment, storage, pest control, food safety officer |
| Fire safety inspection | Fire and rescue | Escape routes, extinguishers, kitchen fire suppression, materials |
| Environmental approval | Ecology and environment bureau | Exhaust, grease trap, noise, waste |

For alcohol, the food licence must include the alcohol category; some cities issue a separate 酒类许可.

## The premises are the licence

The food licence is issued against a specific layout: raw and cooked areas separated, a washing station for utensils, refrigeration, storage off the floor, a changing area for staff, and a ventilation route to the roof or a treated exhaust.

Before signing a lease, check:

- **Zoning.** Commercial use only; residential and some mixed-use buildings are refused.
- **Exhaust.** Is there a riser to the roof, or will the landlord allow one? A restaurant without an exhaust route is a takeaway counter.
- **Grease trap and drainage.** Required, and often a landlord responsibility that is quietly not provided.
- **Power capacity.** Kitchens draw far more than office units; confirm the load.
- **Fire.** Escape width, sprinklers, and whether the unit needs a new fire plan.
- **Neighbours.** Noise and smell complaints from residents above are the most common cause of closure.

:::kill
Do not sign a five-year lease because the rent is good. A unit that cannot obtain the food licence is worth zero, and the deposit and fit-out are sunk.
:::

## Fit-out and cost

For a 100 m² restaurant in a tier-one city:

| Item | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Rent, per m² per day | ¥5–15 |
| Deposit | 2–3 months |
| Fit-out, per m² | ¥2,000–5,000 |
| Kitchen equipment | ¥100,000–400,000 |
| Licences and agency | ¥10,000–40,000 |
| First inventory and staff | ¥50,000–150,000 |

The fit-out is where budgets break: fire, exhaust and drainage work is quoted after the lease is signed and cannot be deferred.

## Staff

- Every food handler needs a health certificate (健康证), renewed annually.
- The company must appoint a food safety officer (食品安全管理员), trained and tested.
- Employment contracts and social insurance are required as in any business.
- Foreign staff need work permits; the kitchen is a normal workplace for permit purposes.

## Online delivery

Delivery is a separate channel with its own rules:

- The platform must display the food business licence and the operator's details.
- A dedicated delivery menu, packaging and temperature control are expected.
- "Cloud kitchens" (共享厨房) can host several brands in one licensed premises, but each brand must be registered against that licence.
- Reviews and complaint handling are the reputational risk; platforms penalise cancellations and food-safety complaints quickly.

## Alcohol

Selling alcohol requires the alcohol category on the food licence, and imported alcohol needs a Chinese label and a compliant importer. Licensing hours and underage rules apply, and a bar in a residential building faces the same neighbour problem with more noise.

## Franchising

If the plan is to franchise, the brand owner must file a commercial franchise record (商业特许经营备案) and disclose the required information to franchisees. Operating two successful stores is not a franchise system; the filing and the disclosure come first.

:::checklist
- Premises confirmed as commercial zoning, with an exhaust route
- Floor plan reviewed informally with the district regulator before the lease
- Food licence scope includes the actual menu and alcohol
- Fire inspection booked before opening, not after
- Grease trap, drainage and power capacity verified with the landlord
- Food safety officer appointed and trained
- Staff health certificates obtained
- Delivery-channel registration and packaging ready
- Franchise filing considered if the model will be replicated
:::
