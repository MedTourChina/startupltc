Education is one of the sectors where the negative list and the education ministry's own rules point in the same direction: the classroom is treated as a matter of public interest, not a service business.

## The negative list position

| Segment | Foreign investment |
|---|---|
| Compulsory education (primary and junior secondary) | Prohibited |
| Preschool, general high school, higher education | Only through Sino-foreign cooperative schools, Chinese side leading |
| Academic-subject tutoring for compulsory-education students | Effectively prohibited for foreign capital |
| Non-academic training (arts, sport, science) | Permitted, licensed |
| Adult and corporate training | Permitted as ordinary company activity |

"Sino-foreign cooperative school" (中外合作办学) is a specific legal form: a contract with a Chinese partner, approval from the education authority, Chinese majority control and a Chinese legal representative. It is not a WFOE with a licence attached.

## The 双减 policy

The July 2021 policy ("double reduction") reshaped the tutoring market:

- No new approvals for off-campus academic-subject training institutions serving compulsory-education students.
- Existing academic-subject institutions must register as non-profit.
- Foreign capital may not control or hold equity in them, including through VIEs.
- Listed companies may not raise money for them, and capitalisation is barred.
- Fees are government-guided, and prepaid fees are capped.

The practical result is that the largest segment of the education market is closed to a foreign founder. What survived is non-academic training — music, art, sport, coding for children — and adult or corporate training.

:::warn
Rebranding academic tutoring as "thinking skills" or "study coaching" is a common workaround and an enforcement target. Inspectors read the curriculum and the marketing, not the category name on the licence.
:::

## Non-academic training

A children's art or sport school can be for-profit, but it needs:

- A school licence (办学许可证) from the district education bureau, in addition to the business licence.
- Premises that meet fire, safety, area and floor requirements — usually no higher than the third floor, with two exits.
- Qualified teachers, with criminal-record checks.
- A capital contribution and a risk deposit, varying by city.
- Prepaid tuition limited to three months or 60 sessions, and paid into a supervised account.

This is a licensing business, not a software business. Budget six to twelve months and a local partner who has run a school before any revenue.

## Adult and corporate training

The lighter route is a company that provides training without issuing recognised qualifications:

- Business scope: 教育科技 (education technology), 企业管理咨询 (management consulting), 文化艺术交流 (cultural and artistic exchange).
- No school licence, because it is not a "private school" and does not enrol minors for academic subjects.
- Cannot call itself a 学校, cannot award degrees or certificates that imply official recognition, and cannot advertise results.

Corporate training, executive education delivered with a foreign university, and professional exam prep sit close to the line. If the programme implies a qualification, the education authority will treat it as a school.

## Foreign teachers

Language schools hire foreign teachers, and the rules are strict:

- Native speaker of the language taught, or a degree taught in that language.
- Bachelor's degree or above.
- A recognised teaching qualification (TEFL/TESOL/CELTA) or two years of teaching experience.
- Clean criminal record, authenticated.
- A work permit for the teaching role and a residence permit.

Some cities add a 120-hour TEFL minimum and a requirement that the employer hold a school licence. Teachers on tourist visas or doing "language exchange" are unauthorised employment, and the school carries the penalty.

## The cooperative school route

If the target is a kindergarten, high school or university programme:

1. Find a Chinese partner with an existing school or a land/premises arrangement.
2. Agree the cooperative school contract: contributions, governance, fee levels, duration (usually 10–30 years), winding up.
3. Apply to the provincial education department; higher education goes through the Ministry of Education.
4. Obtain the cooperative school licence, then register the entity.
5. Operate under a Chinese-majority board and a Chinese legal representative.

Approval is discretionary and slow — often 12–24 months — and the curriculum,教材 and teacher list are reviewed.

## What to check before committing

:::checklist
- Segment confirmed against the negative list and the 双减 policy
- School licence requirement confirmed in writing by the district bureau
- Premises inspected against the local school-site standard
- Prepaid fee cap and supervised account designed into the business model
- Foreign teachers' qualifications and permits checked before hiring
- For cooperative schools: partner, control terms and approval path agreed first
:::
