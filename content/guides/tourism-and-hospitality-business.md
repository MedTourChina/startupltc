Tourism is one of the more open service sectors: foreign brands run hotels, and foreign-owned travel businesses serve inbound travellers. The difficulty is not ownership, it is the pile of premises-level permits and the outbound restriction.

## Travel agencies

A travel agency needs a **travel agency business licence** (旅行社业务经营许可证) from the culture and tourism authority, plus a deposit lodged with a designated bank.

| Scope | Capital | Deposit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic and inbound tourism | ≥¥300,000 | ¥200,000 | The standard entry point |
| Outbound tourism | Higher | ¥1,400,000 | Requires prior inbound operation with a clean record; not automatic |
| Foreign-invested agency | As above | As above | Domestic and inbound permitted; outbound limited to a pilot list of joint ventures |

Practical notes:

- The licence is issued against a registered address and a qualified manager; a virtual address will not pass.
- Outbound business is the profitable part for many agencies, and it is exactly the part that is restricted. Plan the model around inbound and domestic first.
- Online sales add ICP compliance: a commercial travel platform is an internet information service, and paid transactions require the appropriate filing or licence.
- Deposit rules and the outbound pilot list change; confirm with the provincial culture and tourism bureau before signing a lease.

## Hotels

A hotel needs, at minimum:

1. **Business licence** with the right scope.
2. **Special industry licence** (特种行业许可证) from the public security bureau — the hotel-specific permit.
3. **Fire safety inspection** and acceptance.
4. **Health permit** for anything involving food or public facilities.
5. **Food business licence** for restaurants and breakfast service.

Foreign-invested hotels are permitted; the brand and management contract are not restricted. What trips founders up:

- The building's use must be commercial and the fire systems must match the intended occupancy.
- Guest registration must be reported to the public security system; foreign guests are registered within 24 hours of check-in. A hotel that skips this is exposed, not just fined.
- Renovation of an existing hotel may inherit an old fire approval that does not cover the new layout; re-approval is the safe route.

## Homestays and guesthouses

- Rules are local. Many cities require a **special industry licence**, fire equipment, and neighbour consent; some rural areas have simplified registration.
- Operating a homestay without the local permit is a common and expensive mistake: the platform delists you, the district inspects, and the lease may be residential.
- If the property is residential, check whether the city allows 住改商 (residential-to-commercial use) and what the building's management requires.

## Tour guides

- The guide certificate (导游证) is issued to Chinese citizens who pass the national exam. A foreign founder cannot hold one.
- Foreign-language guides are scarce and valuable; the practical model is to employ licensed Chinese guides who speak the language, or to use freelance licensed guides.
- Tour leaders for outbound groups have separate requirements.

## Online travel and platforms

- Selling travel services online is an internet information service: ICP filing for a simple site, an ICP licence where the platform charges for services or hosts third-party sellers.
- Platform obligations under the E-commerce Law apply to marketplace models: seller verification, records, complaint handling.
- Reviews and marketing claims are subject to advertising rules — "the best hotel in China" is not a permissible claim.

## What the numbers look like

| Item | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Travel agency deposit | ¥200,000 (domestic/inbound) |
| Time to licence | 2–4 months after incorporation |
| Hotel special industry licence | 1–3 months, after fire acceptance |
| Fit-out for a 50-room hotel | ¥3,000–8,000/m² depending on standard |
| Licensed English-speaking guide | ¥500–1,200/day |

## The structural choice

- **Inbound tour operator**: foreign-owned agency, Chinese licensed guides, foreign marketing. The cleanest entry.
- **Hotel**: foreign-owned operating company, management contract or brand licence, premises permits in the local company's name.
- **Platform**: ICP-compliant entity, seller agreements, and a payment and refund process that works for foreign cards.
- **Outbound**: needs a Chinese JV partner on the pilot list; treat it as a later-stage expansion, not a first step.

:::warn
Tourism is a sector where "we will sort the licence later" is common and costly. The deposit is recoverable, the lease is not — sign nothing until the licence path is confirmed with the local bureau.
:::
