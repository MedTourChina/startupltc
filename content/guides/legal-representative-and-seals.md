Foreign founders spend weeks on the shareholding table and minutes on two decisions that determine who actually controls the company: who is the legal representative, and who holds the seals.

Both are structural. Both are hard to undo once the company is operating.

## What the legal representative is

The legal representative (法定代表人) is the natural person who represents the company in legal matters. Under the Company Law as revised in 2023 and effective **1 July 2024**, the role must be held by a **director or manager who executes company affairs** — not an outside nominee, and not an employee with no management role.

The legal representative's signature binds the company. A contract signed by the legal representative, or under a company seal, is the company's act. The company can limit the role internally in its articles, but those limits generally cannot be raised against a third party who dealt with the company in good faith.

:::warn
A nominee legal representative is a bad trade. If the company is sued or fails to pay, the restrictions fall on the nominee — but so does the practical control, because the nominee can sign contracts and change bank mandates. You cannot have the signature without the person.
:::

## The personal exposure

The legal representative is not personally liable for the company's debts — the company is a separate legal person. But the role carries specific exposures:

- **Restriction on high consumption (限制高消费)** if the company fails to pay a judgment: no business-class flights, no high-speed rail first class, no luxury hotels, restrictions on children's private school fees.
- **Dishonest judgment debtor list (失信被执行人)** in serious cases: publication, restrictions on credit and government procurement.
- **Administrative liability** for certain company violations — false registration, failure to file, tax irregularities — where the representative is the named responsible person.
- **Exit restrictions** in some tax and customs cases.

For a foreign legal representative, the practical consequence is that the role complicates travel and banking while a dispute is unresolved.

## Who can be the legal representative

- A Chinese national or a foreigner with the right to work in China.
- Must be a director or manager under the articles.
- Cannot be a person disqualified from being a director or senior manager — for example, someone subject to an enforcement ban, or someone who was personally responsible for a company that went bankrupt.

A foreign legal representative who works in China needs a work permit and residence permit in the ordinary way. Holding the legal representative role is not itself a work authorisation.

## Changing the legal representative

Under the 2023 Company Law, if the director or manager who serves as legal representative resigns, they are treated as resigning the legal representative role at the same time, and the company must appoint a replacement **within 30 days**.

The registry change needs a shareholder resolution, an amended article if the appointment basis changes, and a filing. In practice it takes one to three weeks — but the old representative remains on record until the change is filed, so the handover should be documented and dated.

:::note
If a founder leaves and the company does not change the legal representative, the departing person stays exposed to the company's filings and judgments. Resignation without a registry change is not an exit.
:::

## The five seals

Chinese companies use chops rather than handwritten signatures for most formal acts. The standard set:

| Seal | Chinese | Used for | Holder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company seal | 公章 | Contracts, official letters, filings, bank documents | Company |
| Finance seal | 财务专用章 | Bank payments, accounting documents, tax filings | Finance |
| Legal representative's seal | 法人章 | Bank mandates, some filings | The legal representative personally |
| Contract seal | 合同专用章 | Contracts only, where the company uses one | Sales or admin |
| Invoice seal | 发票专用章 | Fapiao | Finance |

Seals are carved by licensed carvers and filed with the public security bureau (公安备案). Cost is modest — roughly ¥200–800 for a set — but the legal effect is not.

## Why seals matter more than the cap table

A contract bearing the company seal binds the company. The seal does not know who the majority shareholder is. If a minority shareholder, a local manager or a former employee holds the company seal and the bank mandate, they can sign contracts and move money.

Founders who discover this after a dispute usually find that the articles gave them a veto they cannot enforce, because enforcement requires a court and the money has already moved.

Practical controls:

- Keep the company seal and the finance seal in different hands.
- Keep the legal representative's seal with the legal representative, not with the finance team.
- Require two signatures on bank payments where the bank supports it.
- Record seal use in a register: date, document, signatory, purpose.
- Do not leave seals with an agent "for convenience" after registration.

:::warn
Handing the full seal set to a registration agent or a local nominee is the single most common way foreign founders lose control of a Chinese company. The agent needs the seals to open the bank account; collect them the same week.
:::

## Electronic seals and signatures

Electronic seals are increasingly accepted for contracts and tax filings, and some banks support electronic mandates. They are convenient but should be issued under the same controls: named users, approval workflow, audit log.

An electronic seal held by one administrator is the same control problem in digital form.

## The handover checklist

When a company changes legal representative or management:

1. Sign a handover list for every seal, licence, bank U-key and password.
2. Change the bank mandate and online banking administrator.
3. Change the tax system login and the e-tax bureau administrator.
4. Change the social insurance and housing fund administrator.
5. File the registry change for the legal representative, directors and supervisors.
6. Notify counterparties in writing, and update the signature block on contracts.

:::checklist
- Legal representative is a director or manager who actually runs the company
- Foreign legal representative holds a valid work and residence permit
- Seals carved and filed with the public security bureau
- Company seal and finance seal held by different people
- Seal use register maintained
- Bank mandate lists the current legal representative and authorised signatories
- Handover list signed at every change of representative or finance staff
:::

:::rule
The shareholding table decides who owns the company. The legal representative decides who can bind it, and the seals decide who can act today. Structure all three deliberately.
:::
