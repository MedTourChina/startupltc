## What this is

A free, independent guide for foreigners who are deciding whether to start a business in China, and how. It is part of the Lifetourchina family of small, non-commercial reference sites, alongside [realty.lifetourchina.com](https://realty.lifetourchina.com) (buying property) and [invest.lifetourchina.com](https://invest.lifetourchina.com) (personal finance).

The site is written in English because that is the language in which most foreign founders are making these decisions, and in which most of the available material is either a sales brochure or a summary of the registration mechanics. The gap this site tries to fill is not information. It is **order**: which decisions come before which, and which of them can end the plan.

## Who writes it

The guides are written and edited by Maya Chen, who has worked with foreign-owned companies in Shanghai and Shenzhen since 2011, and reviewed by Wei Lian, a corporate lawyer in Shanghai who acts for foreign investors on formation, restructuring and exit. The reviewer's role is to check the legal statements and the dates attached to them; it is not a legal opinion on your situation, and neither of us is your lawyer.

Every guide carries the date it was last checked. Where a rule is in transition — the five-year paid-in rule is the obvious example — the page says so at the top rather than presenting a settled answer that is about to stop being true.

## How it is funded, and what it refuses

Nothing here is paid for by anyone it writes about.

- No advertising, sponsored links or affiliate arrangements.
- No commissions or referral fees from agents, lawyers, accountants, banks or serviced-office operators.
- No lead forms, no accounts, no email capture, no newsletter.
- No analytics that identify you, and no third-party scripts or fonts.

The site is an editorial project. It costs little to run and is not expected to earn anything. That is deliberate: the moment a site like this earns a referral fee, the recommendations stop being trustworthy, and the reader has no way to tell.

:::note
If you have been quoted a fee by an agent who says they are "with Lifetourchina", they are not. This site has no agents, no partners and no referral arrangements of any kind.
:::

## How to correct it

Rules move. Practice differs by city and by district. If something here is wrong, out of date, or true in Shanghai and false in Chengdu, write to [notes@lifetourchina.com](mailto:notes@lifetourchina.com) with the page and the source. Corrections are made in the open, and the page date changes when the content does.

We are more interested in a correction that comes with the document — a published rule, a registry notice, a written answer from a bank — than in an opinion. That is also the standard the site holds itself to: see [how to verify](/verify/).

## What it is not

- Not legal, tax or accounting advice for your situation.
- Not a registration service, and not a recommendation of any service provider.
- Not a prediction that a business will work. The framework tells you whether a plan is *possible and affordable*; it says nothing about whether customers will buy.

The site exists to help you spend money on advice only after you know what to ask. If it saves you one bad engagement, it has done its job.
