Where a page describes a rule, the authoritative source is the rule itself — the published law, the regulation, or the written answer of the authority that decides your case. If a page and an official document disagree, the official document governs.

The tools on this site apply assumptions that are stated on the page. They do not know your sector, your premises, your payroll, your tax residency, your treaty position or your bank's practice. Their output is an estimate for planning a conversation, not a quotation, an assessment or a tax computation.
