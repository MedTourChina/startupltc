## The short version

This site collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no forms, no cookies set by this site, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party scripts or fonts.

## What is not collected

- No names, email addresses, phone numbers or company details.
- No form submissions — there are no forms on the site.
- No account or login.
- No tracking identifiers, fingerprints or advertising profiles.
- No server-side logs kept by this project: the site is served as static files by its hosting provider, which may keep standard request logs under its own policy.

## What is stored in your browser

The decision sheet stores your ticks in your browser's `localStorage`, under the key `ltc-startup-decision-v1`. It stays on your device, is not transmitted anywhere, and is cleared when you press **Clear all** or clear your browser data.

The calculators do not store anything. They read the values you type, compute in the page, and forget them when you close the tab.

Your browser may cache the site's pages and assets in the ordinary way. That is a performance feature of your browser, not a data collection by this site.

## External links

Guides link to official registries and other reference sites. Following a link takes you to a site with its own privacy policy, over which this project has no control.

Corrections, questions and privacy enquiries go to [notes@lifetourchina.com](mailto:notes@lifetourchina.com). Email sent to that address is kept only long enough to answer it.
